# 1. Game Overview

**You are a show producer for the annual Magical Animal Carnival.**

Recruit and raise Magical Animals from around the world, each with its own Element and Family, then choose the best performers to take the stage.

Make the most of the Synergies created by your performing Magical Animals and compete with your rivals in two categories: **Cute** and **Cool**.

**Produce the greatest show over the six-day carnival and rise to the top of the Magical Animal Carnival!**

---

# 2. Player Count and Card Composition

## Base Set

One Base Set supports **2–5 players**.

There are 24 different Magical Animals, with 8 copies of each.

**24 different Magical Animals × 8 copies = 192 cards**

## Playing with 6 or More Players

With a second copy of the same Base Set, the game supports **6–10 players**.

When using two sets, combine all copies of each Magical Animal together.

> 1 set: 2–5 players  
> 2 sets: 6–10 players

---

# 3. Magical Animal Cards

Each Magical Animal has two traits used to determine Synergies: an **Element** and a **Family**.

Each Magical Animal card mainly contains the following information:

- Magical Animal
- Element
- Family
- Cute
- Cool
- Cute / Cool values at ☆1 / ☆2 / ☆3

## Elements

The Base Set contains six Elements:

- Fire
- Water
- Lightning
- Dark
- Light
- Earth

## Families

The Base Set contains six Families:

- Dog
- Cat
- Bird
- Squirrel
- Rabbit
- Bear

# 4. Setup

## 4.1 Create the Starting Deck

Take **4 copies of each of the 24 Magical Animals**.

Shuffle these 96 cards thoroughly and place them face down as the deck.

**24 different Magical Animals × 4 copies = 96 cards**

The remaining cards are used to replenish the deck.

Treat the replenishment cards as complete sets of:

**1 copy of each of the 24 Magical Animals = 1 set**

When playing with one box, you can prepare four replenishment sets in addition to the starting deck.

When playing with two boxes, the starting deck is still made from 4 copies of each Magical Animal, for a total of 96 cards. Use all remaining cards as replenishment cards.

## 4.2 Create the Discard Pile

Create a face-down **discard pile** near the deck.

The **number of cards in the discard pile is public information**, but players may not look at the cards themselves.

## 4.3 Choose the Draft Direction

At the beginning of the game, choose one of the following as the draft direction:

- Clockwise
- Counterclockwise

The direction does not change for the rest of the game.

## 4.4 Prepare to Record Scores

Prepare a way to record the following for each player in each round:

- Cute rank
- Cool rank
- Round points
- Cumulative points

Also record permanent bonuses gained from Earth.

---

# 5. Game Flow

The game lasts **6 rounds**.

Each round proceeds broadly in the following order:

1. Prepare for the round
2. Draft for 4 turns
3. Choose performing Magical Animals
4. Check synergies
5. Calculate Cute / Cool
6. Determine Cute / Cool rankings
7. Record round points

After Round 6, determine the final standings using cumulative points.

---

# 6. Round Preparation

## 6.1 Check Element Synergies from the Previous Round

Element Synergies are used **in the round after they are achieved**.

There are no Element Synergies available in Round 1.

## 6.2 Add Earth Bonuses

If you achieved an Earth Synergy in the previous round, add the Earth bonus corresponding to the current round to both Cute and Cool.

Once an Earth bonus has been added, it remains in effect for all later rounds.

## 6.3 Check the Deck Before Dealing Hands

Check whether the deck contains enough cards to deal 5 cards to every player.

If not, add **1 copy of each of the 24 Magical Animals** from the replenishment cards to the deck, then shuffle the entire deck thoroughly.

If there are still not enough cards, continue adding complete 24-card sets until there are enough.

Then deal **5 cards** from the deck to each player.

These are the starting hands for the round.

## 6.4 Check the Deck Before Dealing Water Reserve Cards

Players who achieved a Water Synergy in the previous round receive face-down Reserve Cards from the deck.

| Water Synergy | Reserve Cards |
|---|---:|
| Water 2 | 2 cards |
| Water 4 | 4 cards |

Before dealing Reserve Cards, check that the deck contains enough cards to give all eligible players the required number.

If not, add **1 copy of each of the 24 Magical Animals** from the replenishment cards to the deck, then shuffle the entire deck thoroughly.

If there are still not enough cards, continue adding complete 24-card sets until there are enough.

Only the player who receives Reserve Cards may look at them.

## 6.5 Check the Lightning / Dark Safety Reserve

Lightning and Dark abilities draw additional cards from the deck during the draft.

After dealing starting hands and Water Reserve Cards, check that enough cards remain in the deck.

The recommended safety reserve is:

**5 cards for each player with an active Lightning Synergy**

plus

**1 card for each player who does not have Lightning active but does have Dark active.**

A player with both Lightning and Dark active does not need to be counted as requiring 6 cards.

If the required safety reserve is not available, add **1 copy of each of the 24 Magical Animals** from the replenishment cards to the deck, then shuffle the entire deck thoroughly.

If there are still not enough cards, continue adding complete 24-card sets until the requirement is met.

> Replenishment must always be done in complete 24-card sets containing one copy of every Magical Animal.

---

# 7. Draft

Each round contains 4 draft turns.

At the start of each turn, all players secretly choose one action. Once everyone has chosen, reveal all actions simultaneously.

## 7.1 Choose an Action

Each player secretly chooses one action for the turn:

- **Draft a Card**
- **Use one available Element ability**

If you do not use an Element ability, choose **Draft a Card**.

It is recommended that players use tokens representing **Draft a Card** and each Element, selecting one and concealing it in their hand.

After everyone has chosen, reveal all actions simultaneously.

Once revealed, the chosen action cannot be changed.

## 7.2 Draft a Card

A player who chose **Draft a Card** chooses 1 card from their hand and places it **face down in front of themselves**.

If other players are still resolving their actions, do not reveal that card yet.

Once all players have finished resolving their actions for the turn, the card is revealed as described in **7.4 Reveal the Results** and added to that player's collection.

A card added to your collection remains there unless an effect such as Fire returns it to your hand.

## 7.3 Use an Element Ability

A player who chose an Element ability resolves it according to the rules for that Element.

You cannot combine multiple Element abilities on the same turn.

Information gained through an Element ability that could affect other players' choices—such as cards obtained—should remain hidden whenever possible until all players have finished resolving their actions.

## 7.4 Reveal the Results

Once all players have finished resolving their chosen actions, simultaneously reveal all cards and other results that must be public.

Information that an Element ability specifically keeps private remains private.

## 7.5 Pass Your Hand

After all players have resolved their actions and revealed the required results, pass your remaining hand to the next player in the chosen draft direction.

## 7.6 Hand Size Across the 4 Turns

| Turn | Hand Size at Start |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 5 cards |
| 2 | 4 cards |
| 3 | 3 cards |
| 4 | 2 cards |

On Turn 4, after choosing 1 card, place the final remaining card in the **discard pile**.

Do not pass hands after Turn 4.

## 7.7 Number of Cards in Your Collection

Whether you **Draft a Card** or use an Element ability, your collection ultimately increases by a net total of 1 card each turn.

Therefore, by the end of Round 6, each player will normally have 24 Magical Animal cards in their collection.

---

# 8. Collecting the Same Magical Animal — Star Level

The more copies you have of the same Magical Animal in your collection, the higher that Magical Animal's Star Level becomes.

| Copies of the Same Magical Animal in Your Collection | Star Level |
|---|---|
| 1 card | ☆1 |
| 2–3 cards | ☆2 |
| 4 or more cards | ☆3 |

You do not combine or discard cards.

A Magical Animal's Star Level is automatically determined by the number of copies of that Magical Animal currently in your collection.

When a Magical Animal performs, add the Cute and Cool values shown for its current Star Level **once**.

For example, if you have 3 copies of the same Magical Animal in your collection, you do not multiply its ☆2 Cute / Cool values by 3.

---

# 9. Choose Performing Magical Animals

After the 4 draft turns are complete, choose which Magical Animals will perform in the carnival that round.

## 9.1 Required Number of Performers

| Round | Magical Animals Performing |
|---|---:|
| Round 1 | 3 Magical Animals |
| Round 2 | 4 Magical Animals |
| Round 3 | 5 Magical Animals |
| Round 4 | 6 Magical Animals |
| Round 5 | 6 Magical Animals |
| Round 6 | 6 Magical Animals |

Even if you have multiple copies of the same Magical Animal, that Magical Animal counts as only 1 performer.

If the number of different Magical Animals in your collection does not exceed the required number of performers, all of them must perform.

If your collection contains more Magical Animals than the required number, choose exactly the specified number.

## 9.2 Secretly Choose Your Performers

Each player first takes all Magical Animal cards in their collection into their hand.

From those cards, choose **exactly 1 card for each Magical Animal** you want to perform and place those cards face down in front of yourself.

Even if you have multiple copies of the same Magical Animal, place only 1 copy of that Magical Animal to represent your performance choice.

Do not reveal face-down cards until all players have finished choosing.

Once everyone has chosen their performers, reveal them simultaneously.

After the performers have been confirmed, return the cards used for selection to your collection.

Performing does not cause you to lose cards or move them permanently to another area.

---

# 10. Synergies

Check the **Elements** and **Families** of all performing Magical Animals.

A Synergy is achieved based on how many different performing Magical Animals share the same Element or Family.

| Matching Performing Magical Animals | Synergy |
|---|---|
| 0–1 Magical Animals | None |
| 2–3 Magical Animals | 2-Synergy |
| 4 or more Magical Animals | 4-Synergy |

If you achieve a 4-Synergy, you do not also gain the 2-Synergy for the same Element or Family.

## Difference Between Element and Family Synergies

**Element Synergy**  
→ Takes effect in the next round.

**Family Synergy**  
→ Applies immediately to scoring in the current round.

---

# 11. Element Synergies

Except for Earth, using an Element ability replaces the **Draft a Card** action.

| Synergy | Uses per Round | Turns Available |
|---|---:|---|
| 2-Synergy | 1 use | Turns 2–4 |
| 4-Synergy | 2 uses | Turns 1–4 |

Track the number of uses **separately for each Element**.

Each Element's number of uses is set for each round in which that Element Synergy is active and decreases whenever you use it during that round. Unused uses do not carry over into the next round.

---

## 11.1 Fire — Choose 2 Cards, Return 1

On a turn when you use Fire:

1. Choose 2 cards from your hand and place them in front of yourself.
2. Choose 1 Magical Animal card from your collection and return it to your current hand.

The returned card may be:

- A card taken in an earlier round
- A card taken on an earlier turn
- A card taken with the current Fire ability

Fire may also be used on Turn 4.

If a card returned from your collection to your hand on Turn 4 remains in your hand at the end of the turn, that card is discarded.

---

## 11.2 Water — Choose from Reserve Cards

A player with active Water receives Reserve Cards at the beginning of the round.

On a turn when you use Water:

1. Choose 1 Reserve Card and place it face down in front of yourself as the card you gain.
2. Shuffle your current hand thoroughly and, without looking, randomly choose 1 card.
3. Place that card face down to the side.

A card placed to the side is not used during the current turn.

At the end of the turn, return it to the deck and shuffle the deck.

At the end of the round, return all unused Reserve Cards to the deck and shuffle.

---

## 11.3 Lightning — Redraw Your Entire Hand

On a turn when you use Lightning:

1. Place your entire current hand face down to the side.
2. Draw the same number of cards from the deck to form a new hand.
3. Choose 1 card from the new hand and place it in front of yourself.

Drawing the new hand does not end your turn. You must choose 1 card from it.

At the end of the turn, return the old hand that was placed to the side to the deck and shuffle.

---

## 11.4 Dark — Draw 1, Choose 1, Set 1 Aside

On a turn when you use Dark:

1. Draw 1 card from the deck and add it to your current hand.
2. Choose 1 card from your hand and place it in front of yourself.
3. Choose 1 card from the remaining hand and place it face down to the side.

The order must always be:

**Draw → Take → Set 1 Aside**

At the end of the turn, return the card placed to the side to the deck and shuffle.

---

## 11.5 Light — Call Back a Magical Animal from the Discard Pile

Light allows you to receive random candidates from the face-down discard pile and gain 1 of them.

### Number of Candidate Cards

At the beginning of the turn, let:

- **L** = the number of players with an active Light Synergy
- **D** = the number of cards in the discard pile

The number of candidate cards per player, **N**, is:

**N = min(4, floor(D ÷ L))**

Include in **L** players with active Light even if they do not use Light that turn.

Also include players whose remaining number of Light uses for the round has already reached 0, as long as their Light Synergy is officially active.

If **N = 0**, Light cannot be used that turn.

### Using Light

1. Shuffle the face-down discard pile thoroughly.
2. Randomly draw N cards as candidates.
3. Only the player using Light may look at those candidates.
4. Choose 1 candidate and place it face down in front of yourself as the card you gain.
5. Shuffle your current hand thoroughly and, without looking, randomly choose 1 card.
6. Place that card face down to the side.

Discarded cards that were not drawn as candidates are returned to the face-down discard pile without revealing them.

Candidate cards that were drawn but not chosen are kept face down and separate from the discard pile until the end of the turn.

After all players have finished resolving the turn, return all unchosen candidate cards to the discard pile face down.

Return the card placed to the side to the deck and shuffle.

If multiple players use Light on the same turn, each player's candidates are randomly drawn from the discard pile without overlap.

The value of N does not change partway through the turn.

---

## 11.6 Earth — Permanent Bonus

Earth is not an ability used instead of selecting a card.

At the beginning of the next round, add a bonus to both Cute and Cool based on the Earth Synergy achieved in the previous round.

| Round in Which the Bonus Is Added | Earth 2 | Earth 4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Round 1 | None | None |
| Round 2 | +12 | — |
| Round 3 | +18 | +45 |
| Round 4 | +24 | +60 |
| Round 5 | +36 | +90 |
| Round 6 | +60 | +150 |

Earth bonuses **accumulate**.

Once added, a bonus remains in effect during all later rounds.

Example:

- Achieve Earth 2 in Round 1 → starting in Round 2, Cute / Cool +12
- Achieve Earth 2 again in Round 2 → in Round 3, add another +18

In this case, the cumulative Earth bonus from Round 3 onward is +30.

# 12. Family Synergies

Family Synergies apply immediately when calculating Cute / Cool in the current round.

## 12.1 Dog — Increase Cool

| Synergy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Dog 2 | Number of Magical Animals performing × Cool 40 |
| Dog 4 | Number of Magical Animals performing × Cool 100 |

Example: If 6 Magical Animals perform and you have Dog 2, gain Cool +240.

---

## 12.2 Cat — Increase Cute

| Synergy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Cat 2 | Number of Magical Animals performing × Cute 40 |
| Cat 4 | Number of Magical Animals performing × Cute 100 |

Example: If 6 Magical Animals perform and you have Cat 4, gain Cute +600.

---

## 12.3 Bird — Reward New Performances

| Synergy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bird 2 | Number of newly performing Magical Animals × Cute 30 / Cool 30 |
| Bird 4 | Number of newly performing Magical Animals × Cute 75 / Cool 75 |

This does not refer to whether a Magical Animal is performing for the first time in the entire game.

Count **Magical Animals that did not perform in the previous round**.

- A Magical Animal performing for the first time in the game → **New Performance**
- A Magical Animal that performed earlier in the game but did not perform in the previous round → **Return Performance**

Both New Performances and Return Performances count for Bird Synergy.

A Magical Animal that also performed in the previous round does not count, even if its Star Level has changed.

Because Round 1 has no previous round, all Magical Animals that perform in Round 1 count.

### Example of a Return Performance

If Lion performs in Round 1, does not perform in Round 2, and performs again in Round 3, Lion counts as a **Return Performance** for Bird Synergy in Round 3.

---

## 12.4 Squirrel — Reward Variety in Your Collection

| Synergy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Squirrel 2 | Number of different Magical Animals in your collection × Cute 10 / Cool 10 |
| Squirrel 4 | Number of different Magical Animals in your collection × Cute 25 / Cool 25 |

Count Magical Animals even if they are not performing.

Multiple copies of the same Magical Animal still count as only 1 Magical Animal.

---

## 12.5 Rabbit — Let ☆1 Magical Animals Shine

| Synergy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rabbit 2 | Number of performing ☆1 Magical Animals × Cute 80 / Cool 80 |
| Rabbit 4 | Treat performing ☆1 Magical Animals as ☆2 for scoring purposes only |

Rabbit effects apply to **all performing Magical Animals**, not only Magical Animals in the Rabbit Family.

When Rabbit 4 treats a ☆1 Magical Animal as ☆2 for scoring, that Magical Animal's actual Star Level does not change.

If Rabbit 4 is achieved, do not also apply the Rabbit 2 bonus.

---

## 12.6 Bear — Reward Element Variety

Count the number of **different Elements** among your performing Magical Animals.

| Number of Elements Represented | Bear 2 | Bear 4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 3 or fewer | Cute +0 / Cool +0 | Cute +0 / Cool +0 |
| 4 | Cute +60 / Cool +60 | Cute +150 / Cool +150 |
| 5 | Cute +120 / Cool +120 | Cute +300 / Cool +300 |
| 6 | Cute +240 / Cool +240 | Cute +600 / Cool +600 |

Both Bear effects and Element Synergies apply normally.

---

# 13. Element Synergies in Round 6

Normally, Element Synergies are used in the next round.

However, there is no round after Round 6.

Therefore, Element Synergies newly achieved by your Round 6 performers become bonuses to Cute and Cool instead.

| Element Synergy Achieved in Round 6 | Cute | Cool |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2-Synergy | +40 | +40 |
| 4-Synergy | +100 | +100 |

Apply these bonuses separately for each Element.

If you achieve Synergies in multiple Elements, add all of them.

Earth is also included.

If you achieve a 4-Synergy in an Element, do not also add the 2-Synergy bonus for that Element.

---

# 14. Calculate Cute / Cool

For each performing Magical Animal, add the Cute and Cool values corresponding to its current Star Level.

Then apply modifiers in the following order:

1. Cute / Cool values of performing Magical Animals at their current Star Levels
2. Rabbit 4 adjustment for performing ☆1 Magical Animals
3. Cumulative Earth bonus
4. Family Synergy bonuses
5. Round 6 Element Synergy compensation

The result is your final Cute / Cool for that round.

---

# 15. Cute Rank and Cool Rank

Compare all players' Cute totals. The player with the highest Cute is ranked 1st in Cute.

Determine Cool rankings separately in the same way.

Ties use **standard competition ranking**.

Example:

**1st, 2nd, 2nd, 4th**

Do not split tied players into 2nd and 3rd.

---

# 16. Round Points

The weight of rankings changes by round.

| Round | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Round 1 | ×1 |
| Round 2 | ×2 |
| Round 3 | ×2 |
| Round 4 | ×3 |
| Round 5 | ×3 |
| Round 6 | ×5 |

Cute ranking points are:

**− Cute Rank × Round Multiplier**

Cool ranking points are:

**− Cool Rank × Round Multiplier**

Add these two values together to determine your Round Points.

### Example

In Round 4, suppose a player finishes:

- 2nd in Cute
- 1st in Cool

The Round 4 multiplier is 3, so:

- Cute: −2 × 3 = −6
- Cool: −1 × 3 = −3

The player's Round Points are:

**−9**

Add together the Round Points from all 6 rounds.

A **higher numerical value** is better.

For example:

**−42 ranks above −55**

---

## Scoring Example

Suppose a player has 6 Magical Animals performing in Round 4.

For this example, assume there are no Family Synergy bonuses other than those listed below.

After adding the Cute / Cool values of the 6 performing Magical Animals at their current Star Levels, the player has:

- Cute: 2,300
- Cool: 2,100

Also assume:

- Cumulative Earth bonus: Cute +30 / Cool +30
- Dog 2 is active: 6 Magical Animals are performing, so Cool +240
- Bear 2 is active, and 4 different Elements are represented: Cute +60 / Cool +60

Then Cute is:

**2,300 + 30 + 60 = 2,390**

Cool is:

**2,100 + 30 + 240 + 60 = 2,430**

Compare 2,390 Cute and 2,430 Cool with the other players to determine the Cute and Cool rankings.

The Round 4 multiplier is 3.

For example, if the player is:

- 2nd in Cute
- 1st in Cool

then:

- Cute ranking points: −2 × 3 = −6
- Cool ranking points: −1 × 3 = −3

The player's Round Points for the round are **−9**.

# 17. End of the Game

After Round 6, add together each player's Round Points from all 6 rounds.

**The player with the highest cumulative point total wins.**

## Ties

If multiple players have the same cumulative point total, compare the following in order:

1. Lower total of "Cute Rank + Cool Rank" in Round 6
2. Round 5
3. Round 4
4. Round 3
5. Round 2
6. Round 1

If all of these are also tied, the players share the same final rank.

---

# 18. Temporarily Setting Cards Aside

Water, Lightning, Dark, and Light may temporarily remove certain cards from the deck or a player's hand.

These cards are placed **face down to the side and are not used during the current turn**.

This mainly applies to:

- The old hand before redrawing with Lightning
- The final card set aside with Dark
- The random card set aside with Water
- The random card set aside with Light

After all players have finished resolving the turn, return cards placed to the side to the deck and shuffle thoroughly.

---

# 19. Card Cleanup at the End of a Round

After Turn 4:

1. Place the final card remaining in each player's hand into the discard pile.
2. Return any Light candidate cards that were drawn but not chosen to the discard pile face down.
3. Return temporarily set-aside cards to the deck.
4. Shuffle the deck.
5. Choose performing Magical Animals.
6. Resolve Synergies and calculate Cute / Cool.
7. Return unused Water Reserve Cards to the deck.
8. Shuffle the deck.

Then proceed to the next round.

# 20. Additional Rules

## Owning Multiple Copies of the Same Magical Animal

When counting different Magical Animals, multiple copies of the same Magical Animal still count as only 1 Magical Animal.

## Effects That Refer to the Number of Performing Magical Animals

Use the actual number of Magical Animals that perform, not the number of available performance slots.

## 4-Synergy and 2-Synergy

If you achieve a 4-Synergy for an Element or Family, do not also apply the 2-Synergy for that same Element or Family.

## Element Synergies Do Not Change Mid-Round

Element Synergies available at the beginning of a round do not change during that round, even if you gain new cards.

## Family Synergies Are Determined by the Current Performance

Family Synergies are determined from the Magical Animals performing in the current round and are used for that round's scoring.
